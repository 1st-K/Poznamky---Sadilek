
- používáme pro dokončené a nedokončené činnosti
- začalo v minulosti a jsou furt pravda nebo trvající

- často se since a for

- pro slovesa study, live, work **není rozdíl** mezi perfect a perfect countinuous
	>I've <u>lived</u> in England for 3 years.
			==
	>I've <u> been living</u> in England for 3 years.


>	I've <u>studied</u> English since 2017.
>			==
>	I've <u>been studying</u> English since 2017.

---
## Prsent perfect

#### positive

have / has + past participle
> I have worked.

#### negative

have / has + not + past participle
> I have not worked.

#### question

have / has + subject + past participle
> Have i worked?

### Since & for 

> I <u>have lived</u> in England for 3 years.

### Yet & already

 > I have seen the film <u>already</u>.
 > I've <u>already</u> seen the film.

> ~~I have seen already the film~~

> Have you seen the film <u>yet</u>?
### rozdíly

 1. je neutrální ohledně délky času který uběhl
	> She <u>has worked</u> for hours.
	
	- bez důrazu na tu délku času
	
2. používá se na počet
	> She <u>has eaten</u> 3 pieces of toast this morning.

3. soustředí se na dokončení činnosti
	> I've <u>watched</u> the TV series you recommended
	- říkám že jsem to už dokoukal, hotovo

---
## Present perfect countinuous

#### positive

have / has + been + verb-ing
> I have been working.

#### negative

have / has + not + been + verb-ing 
> I have not been working.

#### question

have / has + subject + been + verb-ing
> Have i been working?


### jak to funguje se stative verbs 

- **nemohu** používat se [[stative verbs]]

> I've known her for years.
> ~~I've been knowing her for years.~~ 

> I've been here for a while
> ~~I've been being here for a while 

### Since & for

> I <u>have been studying</u> English since 2003.

### rozdíly

1. může být použit k zdůrazňení délky času který uběhl
	> She <u>has been working</u> for hours! 
	
	- důraz na tu trvající dobu

2. **nelze** se používat na počet
	> ~~She <u>has been eating</u> 3 pieces of toast this morning.~~ 

3. často se soustředí na samotnou akci
	>I've <u>been watching</u> the TV series zou recommended.
- dávám najevo že se stále koukám, neskončil jsem

4. můžeme používat na dočasné situace, nezvyklé
	> I've <u>been sleeping</u> badly.
	- normálně spím dobře

	> Normally i buy lunch at the canteen, but i have <u>been bringing</u> lunch from home
